Reader

New articles go to their queue folder below (manual captures to Inbox, imported entries to Feed). Choose where new feed subscription notes are created. Applying settings never moves existing files or changes their Markdown.

Article queue folders

An article’s folder determines its reading list, even when its YAML status disagrees. The four folders must be distinct and cannot overlap or be inside Templates or .trash. Reader moves articles when you explicitly change their status; applying settings does not move existing notes.

Try the After flow: focus a folder field, type “Library”, use ↓ and Enter, then Apply mappings. For a new path, type it directly and Apply.
In this simulation, Apply updates only the displayed demo state. No Reader setting or note is changed.
