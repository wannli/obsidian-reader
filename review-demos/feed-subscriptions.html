Try the after flow

  1. Enter https://field-notes.example/feed.xml and press Enter. The subscription appears without importing articles.
  2. Expand a subscription, edit Title and Filename prefix, then press Enter in either field or click Save.
  3. Focus Pause feed and press Enter. It pauses that feed; it does not save the text fields.
  4. Click Sync to show progress and a synthetic import result. A feed URL containing error shows an in-place failure.

“Open note” and “Unsubscribe” are simulated locally. Reset restores the starting data.
