    What this models
    • Toolbar status and desktop S/L/A move the article and advance within its original browsing queue; at the end, Reader returns to that list.
    • The existing palette “Change article status…” opens a picker and keeps the same article open. The proposal adds direct bindable Inbox/Later/Feed/Archive palette commands that also keep it open.
    • Queue is determined by folder, even if YAML disagrees. A Reader move writes YAML status then relocates the note. This demo represents a successful move only; it does not model guarded write failures, acquisition or native modal styling. Counts include the demo palette button, not any extra steps needed to open Obsidian’s palette on a device.
